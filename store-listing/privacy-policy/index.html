Privacy Policy

Loudify – Sound Booster  ·  Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Loudify ("the app", "we") handles information when you use our Android application.

1. Information We Collect

Loudify does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app operates entirely on your device.

No account registration is required. No analytics SDKs are included. No data is sent to external servers.

2. Permissions Used

The app requests the following Android permissions:

MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS — Required to apply the volume boost effect to the system audio output. No audio is recorded or stored.

FOREGROUND_SERVICE — Required to keep the boost active while you use other apps. A persistent notification is shown while the service is running, as required by Android.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Required on Android 13+ to display the foreground service notification.

3. Audio Data

Loudify does not record, capture, or store any audio. The app only modifies the gain (volume level) of the audio output mix using Android's built-in LoudnessEnhancer API. No audio content is ever read or transmitted.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may display banner advertisements provided by a third-party ad network. These networks may collect anonymous usage data (such as device type and ad interactions) subject to their own privacy policies. We do not share any personal information with advertisers.

5. Children's Privacy

Loudify is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
