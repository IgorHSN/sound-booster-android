## Short description (80 chars max)
Boost your phone's volume beyond the system maximum limit.

## Long description (4000 chars max)
Loudify is an Android volume booster that goes beyond your device's hardware maximum limit.

With a clean, minimalist interface, simply slide the control to amplify your audio up to +60 dB above the system maximum — no complicated settings, no unnecessary permissions.

**How it works**
Loudify uses Android's native LoudnessEnhancer, applied directly to the global audio output mix. This amplifies any audio source playing on your device: videos, podcasts, calls, and more.

**Features**
• Audio boost from 0 to +60 dB
• Minimalist flat dark interface
• Runs in the background via foreground service
• Compatible with wired and Bluetooth headphones
• Lightweight with minimal battery usage
• No intrusive ads

**When to use**
— Watching videos in noisy environments
— Listening to podcasts with your phone across the room
— Low-volume phone calls
— Any situation where your phone's maximum volume isn't enough

**Note**
Use responsibly at high volumes with headphones. Prolonged exposure to high volume levels may cause hearing damage.
